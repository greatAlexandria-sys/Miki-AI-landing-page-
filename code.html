Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need new cameras or a new POS system? expand_more
No. Miki connects to the IP cameras and POS terminals you are already running via standard RTSP and API/journal feeds.
What happens when Miki flags a transaction? expand_more
An investigation opens automatically with the exact synchronized video clip, transaction receipt, and itemized mismatch attached for immediate manager review.
How is footage and transaction data handled? expand_more
Data is processed on-premise or encrypted in transit using enterprise AES-256 with strict role-based access and automated retention policies matching your compliance mandates.
What does a pilot involve? expand_more
A structured 90-day pilot across a single pilot store, calibrated against historical loss metrics with zero disruption to daily cashier workflows.
What should I expect to change? expand_more
Expect a fundamental reduction in manual surveillance review hours, rapid investigation resolution times, and systematic deterrence of shrink at checkout.
